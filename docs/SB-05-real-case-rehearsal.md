## Verification method

`tests/e2e/real-case.spec.ts` uses the existing Playwright webServer (`npm start`) and four fresh isolated browser contexts. It creates and joins a real room, readies the players, and starts through the UI. Server time, cryptographic role assignment and the default real case source are unchanged. The test detects the randomly assigned murderer from that player's own authorized response, then selects forgery B. There is no fixture injection, accelerated clock or approval bypass. Drawings are automated marks, not meaningful human sketches; the test verifies transport and rendering rather than artistic compliance or deduction.

Assertions cover private real cards and required details, successful photo load and zoom/reset, three explicit drawing locks and one accepted draft auto-lock, identical published drawings/captions in all four sessions, private initial choices, empty final fields, the full real evidence sheet, secret final ballots, correct arrest/score/shared winners, every card/explanation and attribution, and an unchanged result at Complete. Every session's phase history and evidence timestamp are compared with the agreed schedule. Public/private projections and delivered JavaScript are inspected for premature private content; runtime page errors are collected. Raw cookie headers, credentials, storage states and network traces are not saved.

2. For four physical devices, follow README's local HTTPS instructions. Use a certificate trusted by all devices with the exact LAN hostname/IP in its subject alternative names. Start with `APP_ORIGIN=https://your-certificate-host:3000 HOST=0.0.0.0 PORT=3000 TLS_CERT_FILE=.certs/lan-cert.pem TLS_KEY_FILE=.certs/lan-key.pem npm start`. Replace the hostname and certificate paths with real values. All devices must open that same origin without certificate warnings. Check LAN/firewall reachability and same-origin cookies/socket connection. Do not expose publicly for this rehearsal.
3. One person creates the room; three join privately with distinct names. All four Ready, then host Start. Speak in person or over an existing call. Keep screens/cards private. Follow the 45/40/30/90/20/30-second phases without pausing or explaining the solution.
4. Each person inspects/zooms the photo, draws both assigned details, and submits or deliberately leaves an accepted draft. Check Saved/Locked status, Gallery, evidence arriving 25 seconds into Interrogate, empty final ballots, and the final explanation/scores.
5. Record device/OS/browser, certificate and connection failures, whether both clue regions are readable, drawing/undo/clear usability, unexpected saves or lock behavior, and whether anyone sees another player's private information early. Record actual outcomes, not inferred passes.
